# Scientific data and provenance

The public site is a curated synthetic-coordinate benchmark snapshot from a frozen MHT-X source reference, with SHA-256 source identities. Machine files include directed-edge graph data, synthetic independent truth, event statistics and rendered figure integrity records.

The later ternary/clutter controls add three newly generated source-truth fixtures and 12 individual tracking outputs, with their own preregistration and independent human-review visuals. These cases are development tests after inspection, not blinded holdouts.

The full-resolution PNG archive and experimental bubble/particle/MTB/E. coli/RBC data remain on the authorized X: workstation. No actual microscopy frames, third-party paper figures or unpublished raw datasets are required by this public site.
