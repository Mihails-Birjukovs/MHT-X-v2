# Credits and attribution

MHT-X-v2 draws on earlier particle and bubble tracking research and inherited implementations. A complete paper-to-source attribution and contribution history are part of the ongoing documentation audit; this page does not invent article authorship.

This site adapts the Library INM Documentation Site v1.0.0 visual conventions (originally informed by OOC Particle Velocimetry and WUFI documentation). Its typographic graphics are deliberately labelled project placeholders, not authorized institutional logo files.
